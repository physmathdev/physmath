TubeReplacer

Version 2.5.0-1 · physmath
iOS 5.0 – 10.x Tweaks iphoneos-arm Free

About

TubeReplacer brings the classic YouTube experience back to iOS 10 when the modern API no longer works. It fixes the broken Google sign-in flow, restores the signed-in home feed, subscriptions and recommendations, and keeps your search history between launches.

What's new in 2.5.0-1

  • Fixed the recommendation feed showing only one video: every returned recommendation now gets its own unique group.
  • If “What To Watch” (Интересное) returns fewer than five videos, the tweak replaces it with a full global recommendation feed.
  • “What To Watch” loads personalized YouTube recommendations instead of the subscription feed.
  • Search history survives app restarts: the 30 most recent queries are restored when you open search again.
  • Keeps the channel avatar, view count and feed pagination fixes from 2.0.1-5.

What it does

  • Fixes Google sign-in (AppleSpell) so accounts can be added again
  • Restores the signed-in home feed
  • Restores personalized recommendations, with a global fallback
  • Keeps search history across app launches
  • Fixes subscriptions parsing
  • Prevents crashes when scrolling into the next page of feeds
  • Adds preferences for quality and debug options
